Getting Started

This tool helps you organize multiple links across different tabs without typing the "Open in New Tab" commands repeatedly.

Step 1: Click "Create First Bookmark" below.
Step 2: A tab will open. Edit the title and URL to your preference.
Step 3: Save the tab.
Step 4: Repeat for as many refs as you need.
Done: Click the "Cards" view to open all of them at once.
