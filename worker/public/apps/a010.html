3. Learn the Basics

Numerators & Denominators

A fraction represents parts of a whole. The Denominator (bottom) tells you how many equal parts the whole is cut into. The Numerator (top) tells you how many of those parts you have.

Common Denominators

To add or subtract fractions with different bottom numbers, you must find a Common Denominator. The easiest one is the "Least Common Multiple" (LCM) of the two denominators.

Proper vs. Improper

Proper fractions are less than 1 (top is smaller than bottom). Improper fractions are greater than 1 (top is larger than bottom), like 5/4 (one whole and a quarter left over).

Mixed Numbers

This combines a whole number and a fraction. For example, 1 1/4 is the same as the improper fraction 5/4.
